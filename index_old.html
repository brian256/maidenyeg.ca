About MAIDEN

A boutique alternative to the franchise clean.

MAIDEN started as a simple idea: cleaning is care work, not just a task to outsource. We're a small, woman-owned team based right here in South West Edmonton — not a franchise call centre reading from a script. Every visit is planned around your privacy, your space's real needs, and — where it matters most — the comfort of the seniors (and juniors) who live there, whether that's a grandparent who needs a gentle touch or a new mother who needs one less thing to manage. We show up on time, we use products we'd feel good about in our own homes, and we're building something meant to last in this community for years, not quarters.

What's included

Exactly what happens during a visit.

Fresh Start, Maintenance Plan, and Weekly Plan are the same Routine Cleaning task list below on a monthly, bi-weekly, or weekly cadence — whichever fits your space. No mystery about what we actually do.

Routine Cleaning

Every visit, every cadence

  • Kitchen counters, backsplash & sink scrubbed; stovetop and appliance exteriors wiped down
  • Bathrooms disinfected — toilet, tub/shower, sink & counters; mirrors cleaned
  • Floors and carpets vacuumed; hard floors mopped throughout
  • Dusting of reachable surfaces & furniture
  • Beds made (linens changed only if requested); trash emptied

Deep Cleaning

Everything in Routine, plus

  • Baseboards, door frames & window sills hand-wiped
  • Interior windows cleaned (reachable without a ladder)
  • Behind & under movable furniture and appliances
  • Inside oven & microwave; fridge exterior detailed
  • Light fixtures, ceiling fans, vents & switch plates dusted
  • Grout spot-treated in bathrooms

Senior Homes, AirBnB Servicing, Move-In/Move-Out, and Private Office visits are scoped around this same care standard, tailored to the space — ask us for specifics on your call.
