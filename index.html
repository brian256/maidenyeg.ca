About MAIDEN

A boutique alternative to the franchise clean.

MAIDEN started as a simple idea: cleaning is care work, not just a task to outsource. We're a small, woman- and Black-owned team based right here in South West Edmonton — not a franchise call centre reading from a script. Every visit is planned around your privacy, your home's real needs, and — where it matters most — the comfort of the seniors (and juniors) who live there, whether that's a grandparent who needs a gentle touch or a new mother who needs one less thing to manage. We show up on time, we use products we'd feel good about in our own homes, and we're building something meant to last in this community for years, not quarters.

Privacy-first, always

Flexible and same-day scheduling built around your routine, not ours — your home stays your own private space, and nothing about a visit is ever assumed or rushed.

A real eco-commitment

Eco-conscious cleaning products across every job, as a standard, not an upsell — because it matters for the people, pets, and little ones who live there long after we've gone.

Mindful with seniors (and juniors)

Gentle, safety-conscious cleaning for private and group senior homes, and just as much care for households adjusting to a new baby — with materials chosen for the most sensitive people in the room.
